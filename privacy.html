Landmarky

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 16, 2026

Summary

Landmarky does not collect, sell, share, track, or upload personal data. The app has no account system, ads, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Landmarky lets you save a profile name, landmark names, descriptions, categories, coordinates, and optional photos. This information is stored locally on your device using Apple's app storage. Landmarky does not send this information to the developer or to a Landmarky server.

Permissions

Location access is used to show your position on the map and help create landmarks at your current location. Camera access is used only when you choose to take a photo for a landmark. Photo library access is used only when you choose an existing photo for a landmark.

Apple services

Landmarky uses Apple Maps and standard iOS photo and camera features. Apple may process data needed to provide those operating system services according to Apple's own privacy terms and settings.

Data deletion

You can delete landmarks inside the app. You can also remove all local Landmarky data by deleting the app from your device.

Children's privacy

Landmarky is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If Landmarky's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is reflected in the App Store privacy information.
