Landmarky support

Need help with Landmarky?

For app issues, feedback, and feature requests, email pcesnek290@gmail.com.

Location permission

If the map cannot find your current position, open iOS Settings, choose Landmarky, and allow location access while using the app.

Photos and camera

If adding a photo does not work, check that Landmarky has camera or photo access in iOS Settings. You can still save landmarks without photos.

Delete saved data

You can delete individual landmarks from their edit screen. To remove all Landmarky data, delete the app from your device.

Privacy

Landmarky does not use accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
