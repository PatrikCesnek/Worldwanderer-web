Worldwanderer support

Need help with Worldwanderer?

For app issues, feedback, and feature requests, email pcesnek290@gmail.com.

Location permission

If the map cannot find your current position, open iOS Settings, choose Worldwanderer, and allow location access while using the app.

Photos and camera

If adding a photo does not work, check that Worldwanderer has camera or photo access in iOS Settings. You can still save landmarks without photos.

Delete saved data

You can delete individual landmarks from their edit screen. To remove all Worldwanderer data, delete the app from your device.

Apple Maps navigation

Worldwanderer uses Apple Maps for map display and directions. If navigation does not open, check that Maps is available on your iPhone.

Privacy

Worldwanderer does not use accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
